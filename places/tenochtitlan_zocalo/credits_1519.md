# Tenochtitlan, 1519 - sources and credits

Tenochtitlan, 1519: an illustrative AI reconstruction, not a historical record. The camera follows real coordinates over Mexico City. The era geometry is built from the sources below: 5 of 21 elements are placed with high or medium confidence, 16 are low-confidence or illustrative. Images were generated with open-weight video models. The LTX-2.x Community License is free only for entities under $10M annual revenue, so this stack is open-weight, not fully open source.
